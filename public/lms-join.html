☠ RULES OF THE GAME

  1. Pick one team to win its match each gameweek.
  2. Win, and you're through to the next round.
  3. Lose or draw and you're normally out — unless you've played your joker on that pick.
  4. 🛡️ Everyone gets one joker for the season. Play it before a pick if you're not confident — if that pick loses or draws, you survive instead of being knocked out. It's used up the moment you play it, win or lose.
  5. You can't pick the same team twice all season.
  6. Picks lock the moment the first match of the round kicks off — no changes after that.
  7. Last player standing wins. If there's a buy-in, the organiser collects and pays it out directly — the site just keeps track, it doesn't handle the money.
